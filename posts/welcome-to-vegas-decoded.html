Welcome to Vegas Decoded

Aug 8, 2026 · 2 min read

Las Vegas runs on a simple trick: make everything feel like a deal while quietly charging you for the privilege of being there. Resort fees you didn't agree to. Blackjack tables that pay less than they used to. Buffets priced like fine dining. Vegas Decoded exists to cut through all of it.

Every episode takes one piece of the city — a hotel, a casino floor, a happy hour, a "must-do" that everyone recommends — and asks the only question that matters: is it actually worth it?

Why the posts exist

Not everyone has ten minutes to watch a video, and sometimes you just want the numbers before you book. So every episode also lives here as a two-to-four minute read: the prices, the verdict, and the one or two insider tips that save you real money.

The video shows you. The post gives you the receipts you can screenshot before you go.
TIP
Bookmark this page before a trip. Each post is built to be skimmed on your phone while you're standing in a lobby deciding what to do next.

What "worth it" means here

No sponsorship is going to change a verdict. When something is genuinely good value, it gets the green light:

✓ WORTH IT

And when it's overpriced, watered down, or just coasting on its reputation, it doesn't:

✕ SKIP IT
