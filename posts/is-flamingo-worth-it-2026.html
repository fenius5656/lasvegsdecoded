Is the Flamingo Worth It in 2026?

Jul 31, 2026 · 3 min read

The Flamingo sits dead center on the Strip, it's one of the more affordable big names, and it's been pouring money into renovations. So why does it rank near the bottom of Las Vegas hotels? Here's the honest breakdown before you book.

The scores

By real guest reviews, the Flamingo lands roughly #225 out of 255 Las Vegas hotels on Tripadvisor — a 3.0 out of 5 across nearly 50,000 reviews — with a 7.7/10 on Booking.com. That's a wide gap between its iconic status and how guests actually rate the stay.

Renovations vs. reality

Caesars has renovated in phases — a $90 million refresh of 1,270 rooms starting in 2017, and more since. Ahead of the hotel's 80th birthday, it announced a redesigned lobby, a revamped Bugsy's Bar, and Luke Combs' three-story Category 10 venue opening October 2026. The problem: the room quality is inconsistent. Book a renovated tower room and it's solid; land an un-updated one and the reviews turn sharply negative.

If you book the Flamingo, request a renovated Fab Tower room specifically. It's the single biggest factor in whether your stay lands as a hit or a miss.

The verdict

You're paying for location — and it's an unbeatable one, right at the center of the Strip. But between the middling guest scores and the room-roulette, it only works if the price is genuinely low and you lock in a renovated room. Otherwise, that resort fee buys more elsewhere.
