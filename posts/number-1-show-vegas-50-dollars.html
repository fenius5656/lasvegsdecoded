The #1 Rated Show in Vegas Costs $50

Jul 31, 2026 · 3 min read

The number one audience-rated show on the entire Las Vegas Strip isn't the Cirque spectacular with the custom theater. It isn't the big-name residency. It's a magic show at the LINQ — and tickets start around $50.

The only 4.8 on the Strip

Mat Franco holds a 4.8 audience rating across roughly 2,700 reviews — the only 4.8 in the city. Nothing else on the Strip touches it, at any price.

Why it wins

Mat Franco was the first magician ever to win America's Got Talent (2014), and he's held the same LINQ theater since 2015 — a full decade — recently extending through 2030. No masks, no smoke machines, no gimmicks. The whole show is built on improvised crowd work, so the audience drives the tricks and no two nights are the same.

Read the reviews and the same three words keep coming up: funny, warm, personal. People don't rate tricks that high — they rate how a show made them feel.
TIP
The best-reviewed night in Las Vegas costs less than a buffet dinner. Book Mat Franco before a pricey "must-see" and judge for yourself.

The verdict

While tourists drop $200 on spectacle, the highest-rated show in the city is a $50 magic act built on charm and crowd work. For value and quality, it's the easiest show call in Vegas.
