The $39 Magic Show That Out-Rates Cirque du Soleil

Aug 1, 2026 · 3 min read

Cirque du Soleil's "O" is the most famous show in Las Vegas, with tickets from about $162. A magic act down the street costs $39 — and audiences rate it higher. That's not a fluke. It's a pattern.

$39 vs $162, head to head

When you rank Strip shows purely by real audience ratings, Piff the Magic Dragon at the Flamingo scores a 4.7 — and starts at just $39. Cirque's "O", for all its spectacle and custom water theater, sits at 4.5 from $162.

Who is Piff?

Piff the Magic Dragon is comedian-magician John van der Put — an America's Got Talent finalist who's headlined the Flamingo since 2015. His co-star is Mr. Piffles, billed as the world's only magic-performing chihuahua. It's comedy first, magic second, and it lands.

The pattern nobody markets

Rank every major Strip production by verified reviews and not a single Cirque du Soleil spectacular cracks the top five. The most expensive tickets don't win — the shows built on comedy, intimacy, and crowd work do. People don't rate tricks; they rate how a night made them feel.

TIP
Before you drop $160+ on spectacle, check the audience score of a $39–$60 show. On the Strip in 2026, the cheaper seat is often the better night out.

The verdict

If you want the biggest bang for the fewest dollars in Vegas entertainment, a small-room magic show like Piff beats the mega-productions on the metric that actually matters — whether real audiences loved it.
