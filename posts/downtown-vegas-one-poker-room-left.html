Downtown Vegas Has Exactly One Poker Room Left

Jul 30, 2026 · 2 min read

Downtown Las Vegas used to have poker everywhere. In 2026, if you want to sit down at a real cash game on Fremont Street, there's exactly one room left to do it in.

The last room standing

In July 2026, the Golden Nugget opened a relocated, redesigned poker room just off its Rush Tower lobby — and it's now the only poker room in all of downtown Las Vegas. As other downtown casinos closed their tables over the years, the Nugget became the last holdout.

What the new room is like

The redesigned space seats 63 across 9 tables, with a modern touch that tells you it's built for 2026 players: USB charging at every seat. It's a cleaner, more comfortable room than the one it replaced — a real amenity for a downtown that's leaned into value over the last few years.

TIP
Staying downtown for the poker? The Golden Nugget's resort fee runs about $46/night — still well under the $62+ some Strip resorts now charge.
